# Astra Artist Programme

**The short version, for artists deciding whether this is worth a conversation.**

Astra is a tarot app. It is free to use, with no subscription and no ads. It makes
money one way: people buy digital editions of decks made by independent artists,
and the artist takes most of that money. We only make money when you make money,
which is why the split is 70/30 rather than the other way round.

---

## Usage rights

**You license digital display inside the app. Nothing else.**

- You keep the copyright. Always. This is a licence, not a sale.
- You keep every print, merchandise, publishing and resale right.
- The licence is non-exclusive. You can license the same deck to anyone else,
  any time, without asking.
- No derivative works, no adaptations, no new cards, no use outside the app.
- **Every deck in the app is by a named human artist. We do not create, buy or
  sell AI-generated decks, your images are never used to train an image model,
  and we will never generate AI variations or upscales of your art.** That is
  clause 2 of the agreement, ahead of the money, and breaking it lets you walk
  immediately.
- You can end the agreement on sixty days' notice, for any reason. If your work
  is ever misused, you can end it immediately.

## Scope

**Your existing decks. You do no technical work.**

- Send high-resolution images in whatever format you already have. Any naming,
  any file type, any size.
- We do the sizing, cropping, card mapping and integration, then show you the
  finished deck before it goes on sale.
- You approve everything: the card back, any cropping, the resolution, the
  description, and the price.
- Your name is on the deck page and on every single card view, with a link to
  your shop, so readers who love the digital deck can buy the physical one.
- If you have written your own card meanings, we license those too and credit
  them to you, so readers get your interpretations instead of generic ones. If
  not, we use ours and label them clearly as ours.
- Adding another deck later is an email, not a new negotiation.

## Compensation

**70% to you on every digital sale. 80% when your own audience buys.**

- **You set your own price**, anywhere from $4.99 to $25. We advise, you decide.
- No deductions. Payment processing, hosting, file preparation and currency
  conversion all come out of our 30%. The 70% is of the price the buyer pays.
- When a buyer arrives through your own referral link, you get **80%**. You
  brought the audience, so we take less.
- **Your link does the tracking, and your audience types nothing.** You get a
  link like `astra.app/?ref=YOURNAME`. No code, no discount required.
- **The credit sticks to their account, not their browser.** Once someone who
  opened your link makes an account, you are attached to it permanently. They can
  switch phone, clear everything, buy a year later, and it is still your sale.
- If someone else's link sends a buyer to your deck, you still get your 70%. Any
  commission owed to them comes out of our share, never yours.
- Paid monthly, on the last day of the month, no minimum. Stripe, Wise, Venmo or
  Zelle. If you are outside the US we cover the Wise fee.
- A live dashboard shows sales, royalties, how many people we sent to your shop,
  and every payment we have made you.
- Your deck will never be put into a bundle or subscription without your separate
  written agreement.
- Every artist gets the same deal. There is no better rate for whoever signed
  first, and your royalty rate cannot be cut by a later change of terms.

## Physical decks

We take a **5% referral fee** on physical decks bought from your shop by buyers
the app sent you. You keep your pricing, your customer and your relationship.

- It only applies to sales we can actually trace, by a method we agree with you
  first, usually your affiliate programme or a code you issue. **No tracking, no fee.**
- We show you the traffic either way.

---

## Two things worth knowing up front

**Buyers keep what they buy.** If you leave the programme, the deck comes off
sale immediately, but people who already bought it keep it in their library. We
tell every artist before they sign, because it is the term most likely to annoy
someone later.

**We will not oversell the copy protection.** Images are served at screen
resolution, with no download link and no bulk export. But no app can stop someone
photographing their own screen, and any platform that says otherwise is not being
straight with you. Print-quality files never touch the internet, and we act fast
on anything you report.
