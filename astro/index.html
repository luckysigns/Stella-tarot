Your light

The Seven of Pentacles is a slow harvest — and it lands on a Leo Sun that would rather be applauded now. That's the tension to sit with this season: the work is growing, it just isn't ripe. Leaning on the patience your Sun doesn't naturally carry is the whole assignment.

Your tides

The Knight of Cups arrives in the Moon's seat, and for a Pisces Moon he's practically family — the feeling-led romantic who trusts the current. Your instincts are running true right now. When something moves you this week, it's information, not noise.

Your mask reversed

The Hermit reversed on a Sagittarius Rising: the world expects your bright, outward face, but the lantern's been left inside. Some of the tiredness you're carrying is the cost of performing openness. Let the mask slip with one or two people and the rest gets easier.
